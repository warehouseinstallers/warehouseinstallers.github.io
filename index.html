For business

Commercial & industrial installation

From a single row of shelving to a full warehouse or classroom fit-out, we handle the installation so your team can keep working.

Pallet racking

Selective pallet racking for warehouses and distribution centres.

  • Layout check before we start
  • Assembly of frames and beams
  • Levelling, anchoring and beam locks
  • Wire decking, row spacers and upright protectors
  • New buildings and live, stocked warehouses

Shelving

Industrial and retail shelving for stockrooms, back-of-house storage and store floors.

  • Longspan shelving with wood or particleboard decks
  • Boltless and steel shelving
  • Retail store fixtures
  • Levelled, secured and ready to load

Mezzanines

Mezzanine floors that add usable space without moving to a bigger building.

  • Structure, decking and stairs
  • Handrails and safety gates
  • Installed to manufacturer drawings

Logimat & VLMs

Installation of Logimat vertical lift modules and other automated storage systems.

  • Delivery coordination on site
  • Mechanical assembly
  • Ready for commissioning

Workbenches & training labs

Industrial workbenches and furniture for schools, trade training classrooms, labs and workshops.

  • Mobile and height-adjustable workbenches
  • Bench vises and accessory mounting
  • Uprights, power rails and overhead cord reel hardware
  • Full classroom layouts, set up and ready for students
